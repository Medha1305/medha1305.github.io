About Me

Research Profile

Designing flood resilience that performs in the landscape, not just on paper.

I am a PhD researcher in Civil Engineering at Newcastle University studying how nature-based flood management can reduce flood risk while strengthening catchment health. My work connects field monitoring, hydrological analysis, hydraulic modelling, and evidence-led communication so that flood solutions are both scientifically robust and practically useful.

Nature-based flood management Catchment monitoring Hydrodynamic modelling Climate resilience

Current Focus

Monitoring and modelling offline ponds at catchment scale

My doctoral research evaluates how offline ponds and wider catchment interventions influence flood peaks, flow pathways, and system resilience under changing rainfall conditions. A 13-sensor network and CITYCAT simulations allow me to test these interventions with real site evidence.

How I Work

Evidence-led, field-grounded, impact-oriented

I work across the full research chain: designing monitoring strategies, collecting hydrometric observations, quality-controlling datasets, running numerical models, and translating results into insight that practitioners and decision-makers can act on.

Research Impact

Turning datasets into practical flood-management insight

Alongside my PhD, I have delivered quality-controlled hydrometric datasets, flood risk assessments, and climate resilience work across academia, consultancy, and development projects. That mix helps me bridge rigorous analysis with real implementation needs.

What I Bring

A blend of catchment science, modelling depth, and real-world delivery.

Field Intelligence

Designing monitoring setups, interpreting site conditions, and grounding analysis in what is actually happening across the catchment.

Model-Based Insight

Using hydrological and hydraulic tools to test scenarios, quantify flood behaviour, and compare intervention performance.

Sustainable Framing

Positioning flood mitigation within wider co-benefits such as ecosystem restoration, water quality improvement, and long-term climate adaptation.
