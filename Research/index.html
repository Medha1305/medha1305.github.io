Project Overview

Field Monitoring and 360° Site View

This PhD project examines how offline ponds and related catchment interventions reduce flood peaks, influence flow pathways, and support resilient landscape-scale flood management. The viewer on this page presents the current field photo sphere so the site context is visible alongside the research summary.

Monitoring of Offline Ponds

PhD Research - Newcastle University (2023-Present)

The research combines field observation, monitoring infrastructure, and hydrodynamic modelling to understand how nature-based flood management performs under real catchment conditions. A 13-sensor network supports evidence-led analysis of flow depth, rainfall, and velocity, while CITYCAT simulations help evaluate the wider hydraulic response.

📍

Monitoring Network

13 sensors distributed across the catchment for field-based evidence collection.

🌱

Nature-Based Solutions

Assessment of offline ponds as practical interventions for flood attenuation.

💻

Hydrodynamic Modelling

CITYCAT scenarios connect observed field conditions to flood-management performance.
