Project Overview

Field Monitoring and Pond Image Library

This PhD project examines how offline ponds and related catchment interventions reduce flood peaks, influence flow pathways, and support resilient landscape-scale flood management. The image viewer below keeps the new 360 pond captures on this research page while the main portfolio page retains the single original panorama.

Monitoring of Offline Ponds

PhD Research - Newcastle University (2023-Present)

The research combines field observation, monitoring infrastructure, and hydrodynamic modelling to understand how nature-based flood management performs under real catchment conditions. A 13-sensor network supports evidence-led analysis of flow depth, rainfall, and velocity, while CITYCAT simulations help evaluate the wider hydraulic response.

📍

Monitoring Network

13 sensors distributed across the catchment for field-based evidence collection.
